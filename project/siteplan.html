Flavor Journey — Site Plan

Website Planning Document | WDD 131 Project

1. Site Name

Flavor Journey

The name "Flavor Journey" represents a culinary exploration through recipes from different cuisines around the world. "Flavor" evokes the sensory experience of cooking and eating, while "Journey" suggests discovery, adventure, and the idea that each recipe takes you to a different place. Together, they create a memorable brand that communicates the site's purpose: to guide users through a world of diverse flavors and cooking traditions.

Optional domain availability: flavorjourney.com / flavorjourney.org

2. Site Purpose

Flavor Journey is an informational recipe collection website that provides a curated hub of authentic recipes from diverse global cuisines. The site serves home cooks of all skill levels who want to explore international cooking.

The site provides the following services and information:

  • A browsable and searchable collection of recipes organized by cuisine type (Italian, Mexican, Asian, Mediterranean, etc.)
  • Detailed recipe pages with ingredients, step-by-step instructions, prep/cook times, and difficulty levels
  • The ability for users to save favorite recipes using localStorage
  • A form for users to submit their own recipes to share with the community
  • Information about the site creator and how to get in touch

3. Scenarios

The following scenarios represent questions that typical site visitors (our target audience) might ask when visiting Flavor Journey:

Scenario 1 — Maria, a busy working parent

"I want to cook something different for dinner tonight, but I only have 30 minutes and need something my kids will actually eat. What quick and easy recipes do you have that aren't too complicated?"

How the site answers this: Maria can use the search bar to find recipes by prep time, filter by difficulty level ("Easy"), and browse recipes with short cook times. The recipe cards clearly display prep time and difficulty so she can quickly find a suitable option.

Scenario 2 — James, a college student exploring cooking

"I've been watching cooking videos and want to try making authentic Italian food for the first time. Where do I start, and what ingredients do I need?"

How the site answers this: James can filter recipes by "Italian" cuisine, view detailed recipe pages with complete ingredient lists and step-by-step instructions, and save his favorites to try later. The site also provides difficulty ratings so he can start with beginner-friendly options.

Scenario 3 — Sofia, an experienced home cook

"I have a family recipe I've been making for years and I'd love to share it with others. How can I contribute my recipe to this site?"

How the site answers this: Sofia can navigate to the "Submit a Recipe" page, fill out the submission form with her recipe details, and her recipe will be saved and displayed on the site for others to enjoy.

4. Color Schema

The color palette for Flavor Journey is warm and inviting, inspired by the colors of fresh ingredients and rustic kitchens. The scheme uses earthy tones that evoke comfort and appetite.

Primary — Terracotta #e07a5f

Used for: Call-to-action buttons, active navigation links, hover states, accents, and the hero section gradient. This warm coral-red evokes appetite and energy.

Secondary — Dark Navy #3d405b

Used for: Header text, headings (h1, h2), footer background, and the hero section background. Provides strong contrast and a professional feel.

Accent — Sage Green #81b29a

Used for: Cuisine category badges, the CTA section background, and success messages. Represents freshness and natural ingredients.

Background — Warm Cream #fdfbf7

Used for: Main page background. A warm off-white that is easier on the eyes than pure white and complements the food photography.

Background Alt — Light Beige #f4f1ea

Used for: Alternate section backgrounds (search section), card backgrounds, and subtle separators. Adds visual depth without overwhelming.

Text — Charcoal #2d2d2d

Used for: Body text and paragraph content. Provides excellent readability against the light backgrounds with strong contrast ratio.

✓ This document uses the selected color schema above. The headings use the Dark Navy (#3d405b), the body text uses Charcoal (#2d2d2d), the background uses Warm Cream (#fdfbf7), and accent elements use Terracotta (#e07a5f) and Sage Green (#81b29a).

5. Typography

Two fonts are selected for the Flavor Journey website to create a balance between elegance and readability:

Playfair Display

Type: Serif | Source: Google Fonts

Used for: Main headings (h1), section titles (h2), and the site logo. This elegant serif font adds a touch of sophistication and culinary artistry to the site, reminiscent of cookbook covers and restaurant menus.

Sizes: h1: 2.5–3.5rem (responsive) | h2: 1.75–2.5rem | Logo: 1.5rem

Open Sans

Type: Sans-serif | Source: Google Fonts

Used for: Body text, paragraphs, navigation links, buttons, recipe card content, form labels, and all UI elements. This clean, highly legible sans-serif font ensures readability across all screen sizes and is optimized for web use.

Sizes: Body: 1rem | Small text: 0.9rem | Buttons: 1.1rem | Nav: 1rem

✓ This document uses the selected typography above. Headings are displayed in Playfair Display (serif) and body text in Open Sans (sans-serif).
